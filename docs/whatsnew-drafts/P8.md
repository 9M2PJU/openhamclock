Release-owner note: the FT8 tab needs the `ft8.{mjs,wasm,sha256}` assets on the `wasm-latest` release. They appear after the first push to Staging/main that runs `.github/workflows/build-wasm.yml` (job `build-ft8`); until then `scripts/fetch-wasm.js` skips FT8 with a warning and the tab says the decoder is not installed.
