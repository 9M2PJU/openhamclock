Notes for the release write-up (not for the modal):

- Saved areas survive app updates and the `?nosw` escape hatch; delete them from the list.
- Esri, Google, and CARTO basemaps are not offered for offline areas because their terms do not allow bulk download. OpenStreetMap's own tile servers forbid "save area for offline" outright.
